A website for a bounce house rental business serving the Sacramento area. I originally built this in WordPress years earlier, and it went through a couple of redesigns before this Middleman version. For this iteration, I swapped out Sprockets for Parcel.js to bundle the JavaScript — a nicer workflow than what Rails-world tooling was offering at the time.
